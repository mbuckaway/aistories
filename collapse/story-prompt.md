# The Story

As a creative writer, I want to explore what would happen if one or more of the big AI companies could not raise their next round of financing. OpenAI, Anthopics, Xai, and others are over leveraged. None are able to cover expenses with revenue. Some, like OpenAI, do not seem to have any break even in sight. Each company has deals with other companies for hardware, datacenters, software, etc. The tentacles of each is growing. There are a few that are worried about what would happen if one or more of the companies were to fail to raise funds, how long they would last, and what would happen if they could not pay the bills. If OpenAI were to collapse, how would it affect the markets?
