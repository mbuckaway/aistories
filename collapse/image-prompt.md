# Image Generation Prompt — Upstream Unreachable

## Medium Featured Image Specifications

Based on [Medium's image guidelines](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/215679797-Using-images):
- **Minimum width:** 1192px (required for all placement options)
- **Recommended size:** 1500 x 1000 px (3:2 aspect ratio) — works well for both the Medium feed and social sharing previews
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **Max upload size:** 25 MB

When generating in GPT 5.2, request **1536 x 1024** (the closest DALL-E supported size at 3:2).

## Prompt

A man in his mid-thirties sits alone in a small startup office at dusk, viewed from behind and slightly to the side. He faces a single monitor showing an IDE with clean green test indicators in a sidebar panel. The code on screen is sharp and legible in structure but not readable as specific text. The rest of the office is empty — other desks are cleared, chairs pushed in, a whiteboard on the far wall still covered in faded diagrams from a project no one is working on anymore. Through a large window, the pale brown hills east of Silicon Valley are visible in fading golden light. On the desk beside him, a half-finished pour-over coffee and a legal pad with handwritten notes. A single Slack notification glows on a second, smaller monitor — unread. The mood is quiet devastation: everything in the frame works, but the emptiness around it tells the real story. Cinematic lighting, warm amber from the window contrasting with the cool blue-white of the screen. Photorealistic digital art, widescreen composition.

## Style Notes

- Do **not** include any text, logos, watermarks, or lettering in the image.
- Aim for a photorealistic or cinematic digital-art style, not cartoonish or illustrative.
- The man should not be identifiable as any real person.
- Keep the composition horizontally oriented (landscape) for Medium's layout.
- The central visual tension is between the working technology (green tests, clean code, functional tools) and the empty, abandoned office around it — the tool works, the company doesn't.
