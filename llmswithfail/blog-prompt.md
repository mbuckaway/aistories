# There is no Intelligence in AI
## LLMs are flawed, and few realize it

The current media posts are all the rage about how AI will take over the world. How we will all lose our jobs. AI will allow us more free time. The stories go on. However, the people that are flogging these stores and getting the media's attention of the same people that are spearheading the back to office movement, so we can all sit in cubacles, talking to our colleeges not in OUR office on teams, never to associate with people next to us. LLMs, quite frank, are broken. And the people that "matter", don't get it.

As a software developer, I started using AI seriously back in October 2025. 

## Fatal Flaws
- LLMs hallucinate. They are programmed to do so.
- LLMs can be poisoned
  - https://medium.com/enrique-dans/a-few-hundred-malign-documents-is-all-it-takes-to-corrupt-any-ai-ecebe982b660
- LLMs are trained on their own data, the computer science version of incenst
  - https://medium.com/ai-advances/model-collapse-when-ai-trains-on-ai-generated-data-2c4baf60a016
  - The telephone game concept explains it
- LLMs are limited to their context window and have no continuity from one context to another. No amount of attempts to create memory will fix it.
